Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20.11.2026

Memento ("the app") is a quote discovery and sharing app developed by Baki Bacacı. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is stored encrypted by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it).
  • Profile: username, display name, bio, link and an optional profile photo. These are visible to other users.
  • Content: quotes you write, likes, saved quotes, follows, blocks and reports you submit.
  • On your device only: language preference, notification time and your daily streak.

What we don't collect

No ads, no tracking, no location, no contacts. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage). Google acts as a data processor for these services.

Who can see what

"Public" quotes and your profile are visible to everyone, "Followers" quotes only to your followers, "Only me" quotes only to you. Your email is never shown to other users.

Deleting your account

In the app go to Profile → Settings → Delete account to permanently delete your account and all your data. You can also request deletion at bakibacaci05@gmail.com.

Children

Memento is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you may access, correct or request deletion of your data. Contact: bakibacaci05@gmail.com
